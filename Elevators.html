How installation works

Six floors, one build.

Every project climbs through the same six stages, from the first shaft measurement to the handover walkthrough.

01

Site survey & shaft verification

We measure shaft plumb, headroom, and pit depth against the selected system, and confirm structural loads with the building engineer.

1–3 days on site
02

Shaft & pit preparation

Contractors finish the pit, ventilation, and power supply to spec before any equipment arrives, so the install crew never waits on trades.

Coordinated with GC
03

Guide rails & machine installation

Guide rails are plumbed and bolted floor by floor, then the hoist machine, controller, and counterweight are set and aligned.

Structural anchoring
04

Cab, doors & controls

The cab is assembled in place, landing and cab doors are hung and interlocked, and the control system is wired and configured.

Interlock testing begins
05

Testing & certification

Load tests, safety-gear trips, and door-force checks are run and logged, then verified by an independent inspector against local code.

Third-party inspection
06

Handover & maintenance plan

Building staff receive operating documentation and emergency procedures, and the unit is enrolled in a scheduled maintenance plan.

Warranty starts
