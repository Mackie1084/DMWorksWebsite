How installation works

Five stages, one incline.

Escalators go in along a single rising line — each stage builds directly on the last, from truss placement to the final ride-quality check.

01

Truss placement & structural tie-in

The steel truss is craned or winched into the floor opening and bolted to the building structure at the top and bottom landings.

Rigging & crane day
02

Drive, steps & handrail assembly

The drive unit, step chain, and steps are fitted inside the truss, and the handrail band is threaded and tensioned along its full loop.

Mechanical fit-out
03

Alignment & speed calibration

Step-to-comb clearance, handrail speed, and step level are all tuned so the ride matches spec at both landings.

Precision tuning
04

Safety devices & skirt brushes

Step demarcation lighting, skirt-obstruction switches, comb-plate sensors, and emergency stops are wired and function-tested.

Interlock testing
05

Load testing & certification

The unit runs under rated load while an independent inspector verifies braking distance, speed, and safety-device response against code.

Third-party inspection
