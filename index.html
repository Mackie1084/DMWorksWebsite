About Us

Mr Desi Pansacala

DMW Elevator and Escalator Services was founded by Mr. Desi Pansacala, a dedicated and experienced professional from Purok 21, Inamoan, City of Naga, Cebu. With valuable industry experience gained from working with Yilton Elevator Group since 2018, Mr. Pansacala developed strong technical knowledge and practical expertise in elevator and escalator services. Driven by his passion for quality workmanship and reliable customer service, he established DMW Elevator and Escalator Services to provide professional, dependable, and cost-effective solutions to clients.

At DMW Elevator and Escalator Services, we are committed to delivering safe, efficient, and reliable elevator and escalator services that meet the needs of residential, commercial, and industrial clients. Our experience, technical capability, and commitment to customer satisfaction allow us to provide solutions that clients can trust. We believe in building long-term relationships through quality service, honest communication, professional workmanship, and a strong commitment to safety. With DMW Elevator and Escalator Services, clients can have confidence that their elevator and escalator requirements are handled with professionalism and care.
